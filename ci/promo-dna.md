# Promo DNA: what the three viral promos did (read it before you pitch)

The QR card, navigator and diagnostics promos (October 2026, made by hand here: src/qrpromo and src/diagpromo on
origin/promos-2026-10, src/navpromo3 on origin/promo-nav-carplay) went viral with no ads, no creators: diagnostics 100K
Instagram views in 21 h; on TikTok diagnostics 66K, navigator 59.8K, QR 38.5K. A studio film is shorter and never an ad,
but it is built the same way. This file is the yardstick for your three pitches (ci/prompt.md step 1).

## SEE them first (the owner, 2026-10-29: „სტუდიას აჩვენე ჩვენი პრომოები, რომ იცოდეს როგორები გვქონდა“)

`ci/promos/qr.jpg`, `ci/promos/diagnostics.jpg`, `ci/promos/navigator-carplay.jpg`: a frame every 0.5 s for the first
3 s (the hook), then every 2 s, the time on each tile. Read all three before you pitch. What they look like:
- **The hook is a lived moment, close and warm**: a ringing phone with „უცნობი ნომერი“ on a wooden table and an angry voice
  through the speaker; a woman screaming at her GPS at night; an owner fighting with his mechanic. A real-feeling
  illustrated person (face, acting, a styled outfit), warm lamp light, a room or a street at night: never a white slide.
- **The problem gets worse** in the same place (the calls keep coming, the stranger flirts, the notifications pile up).
- **The turn**: black, the phone alone in the dark, then the claim on white („საქართველოში პირველად“).
- **The proof**: the real screens big, one step at a time, cut with the 3D car and the street (the card on the glass, a
  passer-by scanning it, the three reasons, „გაიგზავნა“).
- **The payoff**: the SAME woman, the same mirror, calm: „ჰო, ახლა კი მშვიდად.“ Then the store card.
Make yours as alive as these: a person, a place, light and sound, never a deck of white cards.

## The spine they share

1. **Hook, 0 to 2 s: an EVENT you can HEAR.** A person in trouble mid-moment, its own sound on frame 0 (a scream, a
   bleeped insult, a stranger through a phone speaker). Never a narrator's question over a calm picture.
2. **Act 1: the problem LIVED** by one hero in one place, getting worse three times, 25 to 35 % of the film.
3. **The turn**: a beat of silence or black, then the reveal: the phone rises slowly into light.
4. **The proof**: the feature on REAL current screens, in the order you tap them (the home, the menu, the feature, its
   result), one plain verb a step, held long enough to read.
5. **The payoff**: the SAME hero in the same place, now calm, and a short line of theirs that answers the hook.

## Why they worked

- **Conflict you hear in the first second.** The sound is the hook: a scream, a bleep, a voice through a speaker.
- **One hero, one place, one problem, shown as events** (the lamp lights, the turn is missed, the phone falls), never told.
- **Escalation**: each Act 1 beat is worse than the last (missed turn, phone under the seat, U-turn; lamp, bill, lamp again).
- **Georgian street humour and a human voice** (ძმაო, a bleep), never a poster line.
- **An earned reveal**: silence or black first, then the phone. The app arrives as the answer, never as an ad.
- **Real screens in tap order**, one verb a step, the row pushed in; never drawn UI.
- **The payoff closes the loop** with the hook's own hero (the mechanic who mocked him now nods).
- **A new picture every 1.5 to 2.5 s**, but always the same story, the same hero, the same car.

## The rubric: score each pitch 0, 1 or 2 per row (12 max); keep the best; 8 or more to ship

| # | criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook: an event with its sound by 2 s | a question or a statement over a picture | an event, but silent or late | a person mid-trouble, its own sound, by 2 s |
| 2 | The problem lived | told by the narrator | shown once | shown as events, worse three times |
| 3 | One hero, one place | nobody, or a new person each beat | a hero, but the places drift | one hero and one place, hook to payoff |
| 4 | The turn | the app just appears | a cut to the app | a beat of silence, black or the twist, then the reveal |
| 5 | The proof | none, or drawn UI | one real screen | 3+ real screens in the order you tap them |
| 6 | The payoff | the EndCard only | someone calm | the same hero, changed, a line that answers the hook |

Car knowledge scores #5 on its own proof (the diagram, the real photo, the real sound, the sourced number) and a crazy
story on its photos; both score #4 on the twist. A typed idea is the plan: its pitches are three ways to SHOOT his story
(the hook event, the hero, the escalations), never three other stories.
