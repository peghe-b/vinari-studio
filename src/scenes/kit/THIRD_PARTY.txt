Third-party material in src/scenes/kit/ (the promo kit, 2026-10-19). Everything else here is VINARI's own.

symbols.ts (drawn by icons.tsx Sym): Material Symbols Rounded, (c) Google LLC, Apache License 2.0
  https://github.com/google/material-design-icons (LICENSE: https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0)
  Generated by tools/kit-symbols.py from the VINARI Android app's vector drawables; the few glyphs the app drew itself
  (obd_port, storefront_line, brake_alert, brake_signal ...) are VINARI's own.

motion.tsx, cuts.tsx, ui.tsx: our own code, written after the patterns of remocn (https://github.com/Remocn/remocn,
  MIT License, (c) the remocn authors): mask-reveal-up, per-character-rise, tracking-in, blur-out-up, rolling-number,
  slot-machine-roll, value-swap, strikethrough-replace, inline-pill-takeover, push-through, focus-pull, shared-axis-z,
  slide-swap, scale-down-fade, toast, sheet, stepper, chat-flow. No file of remocn is copied verbatim.

The cloud Claude also reads Remotion's agent rules (https://github.com/remotion-dev/skills, Remotion's own docs), fetched
pinned by tools/ci/setup.sh into the git-ignored .claude/skills/remotion-best-practices/ and never committed.
